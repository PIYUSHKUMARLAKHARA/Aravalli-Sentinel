PROBLEM STATEMENT — Everyday Environmental Crises

Citizens witness ecological crimes daily, but encounter broken helplines, fear of retaliation, and zero tracking.

GDG Udaipur • Problem Analysis
⚠️ No Helpline Access

1. Tree Felling & Kilns

Commuters see mature Dhau trees cut and illegal charcoal kilns smoking, but don't know who the local beat guard is.

⚠️ Fear of Retaliation

2. Dynamite Blasting

Explosive quarrying shakes rural homes and generates silica dust. Citizens fear reporting without anonymity.

⚠️ Night Ravine Dumping

3. Toxic Slurry Dumping

Chemical tankers unload toxic waste into dry streams overnight, leaching into aquifers and village drinking wells.

⚠️ Zero Accountability

4. Complaint 'Black Hole'

When citizens tweet or call helplines, reports vanish without a Case ID, progress updates, or satellite proof.

🎯 TARGET USERS: Daily Commuters & Hikers • Rural Villagers • Forest Beat Guards • District Forest Officers (DFOs) • Environmental NGOs
